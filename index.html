🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

What we collect

Your name, your email address or phone number, and your password (stored only as a salted, scrambled hash, never as plain text). We also keep what you enter in the app: budgets, expenses, savings goals, recurring payments and challenge progress.

Where it is stored

Everything is saved in your own browser, on the device you are using. SpendWise has no server, so your information is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or track you across websites.

Your control

You can change your name and currency in Settings. “Reset all data” in Settings clears your budgets, expenses and goals. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes everything, including your account, and it cannot be recovered.

Keeping it safe

Because your data lives on your device, anyone who can open your browser could see it. Use a screen lock and log out on shared devices.

Changes

If SpendWise ever adds online accounts or syncing, this policy will be updated before any data is collected that way.
